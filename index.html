Save Money & Reduce Food Waste

A digital marketplace connecting budget-conscious consumers with restaurants to purchase surplus food at 50-70% discounts while reducing food waste by up to 40%

Features

  • Real-Time Inventory Tracking: Restaurants can update unsold food items with quantities and suggested discounts.
  • Dynamic Pricing Algorithm: Automatically adjusts food item prices based on time of day and remaining quantity.
  • Geolocation-Based Discovery: Users can find nearby restaurants with available surplus food within a 10-mile radius.
  • Digital Reservation System: Customers can reserve and pre-pay for surplus meals with guaranteed pickup window.
  • Waste Impact Dashboard: Provides real-time metrics on food waste reduction and cost savings for users and restaurants.
